## Practice

### 1. Color
- **Function:** Replaces representational form as the primary vehicle for emotional expression.
- **Method:** Employs vibrant, contrasting hues to provoke immediate visceral responses.
- **Translation:** Adapts high-luminosity RGB digital palettes to pigment-dense physical oils.

### 2. Mixture
- **Physical:** Wet-on-wet integration, direct-to-canvas pouring, and spontaneous blending over premeditated mixing.
- **Digital:** Emulates fluid dynamics and material viscosity through layered digital brush physics.
- **Result:** Unpredictable, energetic mark-making that records the kinetic movement of the artist.

### 3. Temperature
- **Warm (Red, Orange, Yellow):** Advances visually; generates kinetic energy, tension, and urgency.
- **Cool (Blue, Green, Purple):** Recedes visually; establishes spatial depth, stillness, and conceptual voids.
- **Dynamics:** Juxtaposes extremes to manipulate depth and emotional intensity on a flat plane.

### 4. Texture
- **Physical:** Sculptural impasto, heavy oil accumulation, and raw canvas grit.
- **Digital:** High-fidelity normal mapping simulating light, shadow, and 3D surface topography.
- **Purpose:** Visually engages tactile senses, transforming flat surfaces into dynamic physical experiences.
